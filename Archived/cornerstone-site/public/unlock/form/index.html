What to do with this

Take each step and ask whether skipping it would break a law or a contract, put your insurance at risk, or let a mistake through. If it's none of those, that step is a candidate to cut. Change one thing at a time and watch what breaks.

    A step you can't cut can still get faster. A signature by post and a signature by email are the same step at very different speeds. Before you call a wait fixed, ask what's making it long.
