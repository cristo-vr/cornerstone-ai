# About — Cristo Van Rensburg

> For use on the Cornerstone AI website about page and newsletter landing pages. Written in Cristo's voice.

---

I started my first business at 16, selling firewood to neighbors in South Africa.

A year later I was making cold calls from my high school classroom trying to land clients for a company I'd just started. I didn't know what I was doing. I just knew I wanted to build something.

Since then I've worked across hospitality, construction, real estate, marketing agencies, insurance, and franchise operations. Every time, the same problem showed up: teams drowning in admin. Talented people wasting hours on tasks that systems should handle. Founders stretched so thin that growth felt like a risk instead of an opportunity.

That's what Cornerstone AI exists to solve.

I help founder-led businesses — particularly in financial services — double their operational capacity using AI and automation. Not by replacing people. By removing the repetitive, manual, soul-crushing work that eats 30-40% of a team's day.

The name Cornerstone comes from my faith. Christ is the cornerstone of the church, and that's the foundation this company is built on. It shows up in how we work: people come first, always. We don't build tech that looks impressive on a slide deck. We build systems that make a real difference to the actual humans using them every day.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

I once audited a 70+ branch franchise operation and identified R3.6 million in annual returns from a R168,000 implementation. Every recommendation was backed by hard data — hours saved, revenue recovered, payback period calculated to the day.

That's how I work. I come in, map where the hours are going, prove with real numbers whether automation can make a meaningful impact, and then build and implement the systems with the team — not just hand over a document and walk away.

If we can't prove the impact, you don't pay.

I'm 20. I run this alone. And I've spent more hours building AI systems than most people twice my age. Not because I'm special — because I started early and I didn't stop.

If your team is maxed out, your admin is eating your capacity, and you know there's a better way to run things but you don't have the time to figure it out — that's exactly what I do.

Let's talk.
